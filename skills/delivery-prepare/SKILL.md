# Delivery Request Preparation

Read the [project contract](../delivery-workflow/references/contract.md) once. A clear small change goes directly to its existing owner or standalone multi-agent-dev; this role is not routine dispatch.

## Recover before asking

Read the request, controlling PRD/decisions and minimal relevant origin context. Identify settled choices, overlaps, exclusions, active authority and the exact remaining gap. Search stable decision IDs/aliases before reopening a question.

For genuine requirements/design grilling apply installed grill-with-research: complete or verify current industrial, inspected open-source and academic foundation; research material pivots; search prior decisions before each question. Adopt applicable convergent practice as a research default; ask only surviving consequential conflicts or unavailable inputs. State a material contradiction before reopening a settled decision.

Clear intent needs no repeated interview or research ritual. Resolve reversible low-impact uncertainty through bounded inspection or a recorded provisional default. Treat product requirements and untested engineering assumptions separately; discussion length does not establish architecture validity.

## Prepare a usable request

Update existing controlling records within authorization before dependent work. Capture intended user/problem and observable result; scope/exclusions; acceptance surface and identity; settled decisions with authority/status; consequential gaps; known dependencies and the riskiest testable assumption. Verify whether required history, cutoff and completeness are available; do not silently replace historical acceptance with latest-available data.

If specification/tickets are actually needed, use the installed prepare-work-spec and split-work-into-tickets adapters rather than implicitly invoking manual-only vendor workflows. Do not manufacture those stages for already clear work.

If persistence is unavailable, provide a marked draft and precise blocker. Retain detailed discussion in the origin chat instead of duplicating transcripts.

## Introduce to the existing PM

Return one concise introduction with origin task ID/link, controlling records, accepted outcome, material open items and next useful verification. Verify recipient identity before authorized contact; a missing destination blocks sending, not preparation. Creation/contact follow actual tool authority.

PM checks current overlap, feasibility and dependencies without replaying the interview. Completion here means the request/introduction is ready with honest persistence status, not that delivery was assigned or implementation approved. Remain available for targeted clarification; execution requires a separate bounded assignment.
