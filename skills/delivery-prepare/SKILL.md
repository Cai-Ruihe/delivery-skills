# Prepare a Delivery Request

Clear small work goes to its owner or standalone core. Recover intent, controlling decisions and minimal origin evidence. Search settled IDs/aliases before questions; only material contradictions reopen them.

Read [contract](../delivery-workflow/references/contract.md) for new/changed/unclear project record or decision authority. [Philosophy](../multi-agent-dev/references/philosophy.md) applies to value conflict/unfamiliar foundations; [research](../multi-agent-dev/references/research.md) to complex decision gaps. Genuine design grilling follows installed grill-with-research: current foundation/pivot and prior decisions before consequential questions. Ordinary gaps need targeted inspection/provisional defaults, not mandatory research.

Prepare one concise authorized brief: user/problem/value, observable capability and acceptance identity/surface, scope/exclusions, settled constraints, material gaps/dependencies and riskiest testable assumption. Check actual historical coverage and route feasibility; no silent fixture/latest-data substitution. Use prepare-work-spec/split-work-into-tickets only when needed.

Persist in existing records or mark a draft. Introduce to the existing PM with pointers and next useful check; contact triggers core delegation/recovery, not implicit sending authority. Ready preparation is not delivery assignment or implementation permission. Keep discussion at origin, not copied history; do not load sibling roles/background reports.
