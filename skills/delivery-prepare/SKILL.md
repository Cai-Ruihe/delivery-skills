# Prepare a Delivery Request

Clear small work goes directly to its owner or [standalone core](../multi-agent-dev/SKILL.md). Recover original intent and controlling decisions; search settled IDs/aliases before asking questions. Reopen only material contradictions.

Read [contract](../delivery-workflow/references/contract.md) when project authority/records are new, changed or incomplete. Value conflicts trigger [philosophy](../multi-agent-dev/references/philosophy.md); complex decision gaps trigger [research](../multi-agent-dev/references/research.md). Genuine design grilling follows the available grill-with-research workflow and existing decisions. Ordinary gaps need targeted inspection, not a research ceremony.

Prepare one concise brief in existing records: user/problem/value, required capability and entry/surface/identity, scope/exclusions, retained constraints, first real interface, critical dependency and riskiest testable assumption. Include relevant human decision opportunity and resource retention/exit dependencies. Do not silently substitute fixtures, latest-only data or a different user flow.

Preparation supplies intent and proposed acceptance; it does not replace independent QA derivation or create another product owner/PRD. Persist accepted changes with their source; otherwise label draft. Hand to the existing PM with pointers and next useful check. Contact triggers core delegation/recovery, not sending permission. Ready preparation is neither assignment nor implementation authorization. Keep discussion at origin; do not load sibling roles or historical reports.
