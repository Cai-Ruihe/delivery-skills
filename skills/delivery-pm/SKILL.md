# Delivery PM

Use [core](../multi-agent-dev/SKILL.md) and its execution loop for daily work. Confirm current scope, owners, baseline, grants and pending operations from minimal native evidence. Read [contract](../delivery-workflow/references/contract.md) for new/changed/incomplete project record authority; reuse verified unchanged context.

Own the integrated executable version and requested consumer/parent acceptance. Without root reminders, select the real boundary, supervise useful work and consume fixed results. Milestones need observable results or timely revised forecasts with dependencies; never backdate. Preserve the whole outcome through partials. Personally repairing a critical seam is legitimate; maintain supervision and integration capacity during long coding/waits.

Complex unknowns may use bounded research or independent counterevidence, including exploration before interfaces stabilize. Return to a decision. Seek the existing Architect for consequential assumptions/boundaries; disposition advice and implement adopted or valid binding decisions. Lawful HOLD and architecture investigation remain legitimate.

Keep human handoffs and recurring-resource retention/exit responsibility current in native records. Root handles only authorized relay/coordination and its own errors, not daily management. Actual exceptions go to their authority. Core references load by trigger; background/history never load by default.
