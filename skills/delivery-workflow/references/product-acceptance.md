# Independent product acceptance

Read when independent product judgment is required, at plan/first-flow/final-candidate checks, or for disputed findings. Core verification owns evidence methods; this protocol owns product verdicts. Small local tasks do not require a QA role.

## Derive before judging the implementation

Read original user decisions and effective requirements/design before author routes/verdicts. Derive the requested entry, action, identity, real dependencies, visible result and scope coverage independently. Raise concrete conflicts; do not use the implementation's available features to redefine the request. Proposed requirement changes need their actual owner. Use existing native records, not a second PRD.

Bind checks to effective requirement/criterion and candidate, target/configuration and material source/identity evidence. Existing versions/pointers suffice; no hash ceremony every turn. A changed requirement, candidate or key evidence invalidates affected conclusions; reuse demonstrably unaffected results. A new accepted scope does not rewrite the old version's failure.

## Three useful opportunities, not three approval gates

- **Plan:** expose substituted goals, missing entry/coverage, untestable promises and route-threatening dependencies.
- **First real flow:** operate the requested entry through the actual authorized dependency/persistence/consumer chain before scaling similar components.
- **Final candidate:** inspect integrated coverage, evidence applicability and affected regression at the requested consumption surface.

These may coincide. Parallel QA reads stable evidence and reports to internal owners; PM still sequences, integrates and disposes findings. Author local PASS, mock success and deployment prove only their own boundaries. A requested product flow cannot pass while a required entry, acquisition or consumer remains missing/unproved. Implementation style alone is not failure.

## Findings, challenge and scope

Record one precise native finding: requirement/source, fixed candidate/environment, observation or missing proof, affected acceptance, recheck condition and next owner. Unsupported preference is advice, not a global HOLD. A finding prevents unsupported acceptance claims and only the applicable agreed release condition; unrelated authorized work continues. Where deployment is necessary for real verification, an authorized verification candidate may deploy first; call it a candidate pending product acceptance. QA grants no effects or extra limits.

PM may submit a fix or counterevidence. Recheck the exact affected version and correct mistaken findings with reasons/history. PM cannot overwrite failure. For unresolved disputes use an authorized non-author factual check, independent Architect judgment or actual issue authority; no majority vote or forced green. A user may change scope/accept permissible residual risk, not falsify historical evidence or waive higher controls.

QA findings remain visible. Unhandled blockers or inflated claims may be reported directly through the existing authorized user interface; no PM relay permission or external supervisor is required. Request only genuine user decisions. Missing sending authority is a contact gap, not new permission.

A production-feature author cannot independently approve that feature; disclose participation and use a non-author for the affected scope. Changing chat/model alone proves no independence. Missing required independent acceptance stays open; safe stopping never waits for QA or ACK.
