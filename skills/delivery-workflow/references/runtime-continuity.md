# Continuation is a host capability

Read for in-flight results, terminal-PM continuation or host control responsibility. Normal work uses the active PM's supported native read/wait calls. Text and local scripts supply no scheduler, durable subscription or cold wakeup guarantee. No external user proxy is the recovery fallback.

In existing round state preserve pending task/result versions, original identities, continuation-in-flight, cumulative bounds, known effects and next owner/check. On active-call return, reconcile current fixed results before declaring waiting. A completed report may require semantic disposition; an active partial file does not. See [result handling](result-consumption.md) only at adopted boundaries.

Before ending with an outstanding obligation, identify the existing authorized continuation owner and supported observation route. A field naming an owner does not prove transfer; preserve unconfirmed responsibility explicitly. Do not keep workers indefinitely waiting for ACK or force valuable work to end just to manufacture an experiment.

Outside PM turns, only a confirmed runtime owner with a verified supported route may arrange bounded continuation of the original terminal PM. Check same-thread admission, human/continuation races, in-flight operations and uncertain-send queries. Read-then-send is not atomic takeover; stale visibility is Unknown. Without the necessary guarantees, retain the gap and use only authorized reconciliation/notification. Do not create a daemon, queue, replacement identity or private app-server connection.

A native scheduled task, when separately authorized and actually supported, must bind one current obligation, finite expiry, action cutoff and original budgets. Read back its actual configuration. Installation or an upstream source-code feature proves neither triggering nor PM consumption. Active-turn handoff and terminal-turn wakeup are separate tests. A successful sample does not establish reliability across hosts, cancellation or capacity failures.

Never replay uncertain effects to test recovery. Local result markers deduplicate consumption, not remote dispatch. Reconcile after a confirmed failed turn before supported continuation under existing model/identity policy. No silent substitution. Busy writers freeze conflicting work, not unrelated lanes.

At acceptance, lawful waiting, expiry or exhausted recovery, stop the exact owned supervision through supported controls and read back what happened. Preserve queued/late-turn uncertainty, result versions, residual resources and next responsibility. A late turn may only reconcile or perform authorized safety settlement; it cannot revive product work. Task deletion, thread idle and stopped development do not prove every process, browser or bill stopped.

Historical experiment results and host-specific recipes belong in accessible maintenance records, not mandatory runtime context. Missing evidence stays Unknown; do not rerun expired probes or copy old identifiers.
