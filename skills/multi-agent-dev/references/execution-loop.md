# One execution loop

Read for substantial/ongoing work, multiple dependencies or coordination. Clear small local tasks need only the core entry and relevant check. This is the sole execution-loop body; PM and assigned work add role responsibilities without another cycle.

At takeover, changed instructions/results/interfaces, ready work, blocker or integration backlog:

1. **Observe:** confirm current outcome/scope, baseline, owners, authority/bounds and pending operations using minimal native evidence. Reuse only demonstrably unchanged state. Inspect changed results and the first unproved capability boundary; distinguish callable, fixture, uninstalled, incompatible and unavailable authorized route.
2. **Choose:** identify the real route to the requested user capability and its first unproved dependency. Select an action that advances that boundary or discriminates a route-threatening premise, with proportionate risk and lifecycle cost. Check runtime/access/reader feasibility before dependent expansion; diagnose before repairing. Necessary implementation may prepare the slice without proving consumer readiness. If an equivalent package changes neither boundary nor decision, resolve the specific dependency or hold. Legal pauses and useful architecture investigation are not failed execution.
3. **Act:** perform a bounded check/repair, dispatch useful independent work, research complex uncertainty, seek independent technical judgment, or present the smallest actual consequential gap to its owner. Do not wait for root to manage ordinary actions.
4. **Integrate/verify:** inspect fixed results and integrate through their owner. Check the final executable entry, actual package/runtime and known correction together, then the required consumer boundary. Update existing state only for material evidence/decision changes, with actual result, next owner/action and pass/stop. A milestone delivers an observable result or a current revised forecast and dependency; never backdate. Partial remains partial.

On important recurrence without a changed critical premise or real-boundary advance, decide now: continue with a new discriminating hypothesis; narrow the next milestone while preserving the agreed outcome; replace the route on evidence; or escalate the precise dependency. Changing agreed scope or effects needs its actual authority. Do not repeat qualification to defer the decision. Serious structural risk requires independent judgment immediately; [recovery](recovery.md) retains retry/repair backstops and explicit bounds. This is not a new approval or reporting stage.

For needed references use [research](research.md); coordination/contact/review uses [delegation](delegation.md); consequential seams use [verification](verification.md); uncertain authority/operations, repair bounds and stops use recovery. These are conditional links. Keep supervision feasible during long coding/waits; no unsupported wakeup promise. Do not duplicate records, weaken acceptance or reset accumulated limits.
