# One execution loop

Read for substantial/ongoing work, multiple dependencies or coordination. Clear small local tasks need core and a relevant check. Delivery roles add responsibilities, not another loop.

At takeover, changed instructions/results/interfaces, ready work, blocker or integration backlog:

1. **Observe:** confirm effective outcome/scope, baseline, owners, authority/bounds and pending operations from minimal native evidence. Reuse demonstrably unchanged state. Inspect changed fixed results and the first unproved capability boundary; distinguish callable, fixture, uninstalled, incompatible and unavailable authorized routes.
2. **Choose:** advance that boundary or discriminate a route-threatening premise with proportionate risk/lifecycle cost. Check runtime/access/reader feasibility before expansion. Required preparation is not consumer readiness. HOLD affects only dependent actions. Before project-wide waiting, reconcile undisposed fixed results and current valuable actions, their dependencies/owners/resume conditions. Continue useful authorized independent work; otherwise wait legitimately. Do not manufacture equivalent work or search indefinitely to prove none exists. Lawful pauses and useful architecture investigation are not failures.
3. **Act:** perform a bounded check/repair, dispatch useful independent work, research uncertainty, seek independent technical judgment, or submit the smallest actual decision gap to its owner. Internal coordination needs no external reminder or supervisor. Keep milestone and integration capacity realistic during long coding/waits.
4. **Integrate/verify:** consume fixed results; integrate through one owner. Inspect the executable entry, actual package/runtime and known corrections together, then the requested consumer boundary and required independent findings. Disposition findings through fix, counterevidence, justified waiting or precise escalation; do not overwrite them. Update existing state for material evidence/decisions with actual result and next owner/action/pass-or-stop. Deliver an observable milestone or promptly revise its forecast/dependency; never backdate or shrink the agreed outcome through partials.

On important recurrence without a changed critical premise or real-boundary advance, decide now: continue with a new discriminating hypothesis; narrow the next milestone while preserving the outcome; replace the route on evidence; or escalate the exact dependency. Do not repackage qualifications to defer that choice. Scope/effect changes need their authority. Serious structural risk warrants immediate independent judgment; [recovery](recovery.md) retains repair backstops and original bounds, not permission to try until exhaustion.

[Research](research.md) applies to decision-relevant references, [delegation](delegation.md) to workers/contact/review, [verification](verification.md) to consequential seams, recovery to authority/uncertain effects/stops. Links are conditional. No unsupported wakeup, duplicate ledger, weakened acceptance or reset limits.
