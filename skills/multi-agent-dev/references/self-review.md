# Reflection that changes behavior

Read for required checkpoints, sustained work, bounds or corrective follow-through. PM owns project follow-through; standalone primary owns it without PM. Workers supply changed evidence, not parallel essays. Self-reflection is not independent review. Architect involvement follows explicit user requirements or consequential system judgment, not routine approvals.

Recover original authority, start/stop, project deadline if separate, and last/next actual checkpoint in existing state. Preserve the full outcome. An effort limit is not automatically a completion deadline. Check critical dependencies, integration, access and safe stopping against remaining bounds; estimates are conditional, not measured throughput.

Follow the user's exact cadence and continuation conditions. Where formal independent Architect review is required every three hours, retain every node, including HOLD, and record its independent conclusion and PM disposition before required resumption. Otherwise the approximately 60-minute active-work default is a lightweight changed-evidence check, not another essay or approval cycle; small tasks may finish first. Material failure or changed feasibility triggers earlier review. Check time before long calls/waits and on return; use supported bounded yields and stop controls. Invocation creates no scheduler or wakeup guarantee. Never sleep to fill cadence or backfill observations. Reconcile or safely stop uncertain operations rather than interrupting them for a note.

Use the existing record, reusing unchanged context, for a short changed-evidence checkpoint:

1. Newly accepted evidence and what it actually proves.
2. Previous correction's action/result or why still open.
3. Current key uncertainty/blocker and why the route should continue/change.
4. Next action, owner and pass/stop condition.

Keep scheduled checkpoint, observed admission closure, actual pause and owned cleanup evidence distinct. An automatic gate is not proof that PM or Architect woke, reviewed or adopted anything. Missing observations remain Unknown; report a host capability gap instead of inventing a watcher. Context loss or changed instructions may require rereading necessary rules.

Apply a correction to the next affected assignment/check; observed result closes it, not a promise. If recurrence reflects a faulty model/boundary, seek independent technical judgment under [recovery](recovery.md). Legal architecture investigation and user-imposed pauses are not idle failures; absent ready-work supervision or unchecked integration requires an actual decision. Recommendations create no approval gate and cannot waive user/security acceptance.

Before stopping choose risk-based reconciliation time. Preserve candidate/working route, evidence, remaining hypotheses, workers/uncertain effects, next check and authority. Follow [recovery](recovery.md) for stops/resumption. Handback and checkpoint completion do not imply project acceptance. Never extend limits or weaken acceptance automatically.
