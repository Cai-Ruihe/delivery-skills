# Work windows, reflection and corrective action

Read for sustained/timeboxed work or corrective follow-through; reuse unchanged instructions.

## Owner and time meanings

PM owns project reflection; assigned workers supply evidence, not separate hourly essays. Without PM the primary owns reflection/acceptance. Architect challenges material technical disputes, architecture defects or important recurrence after correction, not every hourly note. Self-reflection is not independent verification.

Recover run ID, authority source, T0, applicable window stop, project completion deadline and last/next actual checkpoint in existing state. An effort window is not automatically the project's deadline; they can coincide when explicitly required. Preserve the full outcome. Resolve ambiguity from the original instruction before promising completion.

Assess critical path, proven prerequisites, access/installation, context recovery, waiting and integration budget. Separate active/parallel effort and elapsed waiting; conditional estimates are not measured throughput. Choose an acceptance-changing slice, verified prerequisite or discriminating failure. Complexity explains unfinished work only with evidence; it cannot excuse late known-contract checks, idle ready work, absent supervision or repeated avoidable defects. Project acceptance and window progress remain separate.

## Cadence at safe boundaries

During sustained execution check time at normal boundaries; reflect approximately every 60 minutes at the next safe point. Preserve actual checkpoint time across handoffs; user cadence and earlier stops prevail. Small tasks may finish first. Before long calls/waits compare duration with checkpoint/deadline, use supported bounded yields and check time on return. Enforce bounds with available controls; a prompt timestamp is not a timer.

Reuse authorized reporting/scheduling; skill invocation creates no scheduler or unattended-wakeup promise. Do not sleep to fill an hour. After idle or a missed checkpoint, record actual time/gap and one current-state check; never backfill observations. Do not interrupt an uncertain external operation merely to report; reconcile or reach its safe stop.

Review immediately for material failure, important recurrence or changed acceptance feasibility; do not wait for the hour/five-repair backstop. An unchanged external blocker is not another failed correction.

## Short checkpoint and follow-through

Use existing records/messages, reporting only:

1. New accepted results/evidence, including parent acceptance if applicable.
2. Previous correction's actual action/result or why still open.
3. Current blocker and reason to continue/change direction.
4. Next acceptance-changing action, owner and pass/stop condition.

No quota/repeated background; expand only for unresolved issues. Apply corrections to the next affected assignment/check before dependent work. Evidence closes corrections, not promises. Important recurrence seeks authorized independent challenge under [recovery](recovery.md). Recommendations create no approval gate. Exceptions require applicable authority, bounded scope, mitigation and remaining validation; PM cannot waive user/security acceptance.

## Close and continue

Choose risk-based reconciliation time before the stop; avoid work unable to reach a safe stop. Preserve exact candidate/last working version, evidence, remaining hypotheses/dependencies, workers/uncertain operations, next check and authority. Propagate stops under [recovery](recovery.md); handoff is not completion. Same-window resumption retains limits; distinctly authorized new windows check their own instruction while preserving outcome history. Never automatically extend, restart or weaken acceptance.
